# Mockups

`canvas/` holds the sources of the retained boards, in the `.dc.html` format of the claude.ai design
canvas. They do not open on their own in a browser: they are a reference for the implementation. All
names in them are invented.

| Board | Role |
|---|---|
| `Chibi.dc.html` | the chibi avatar: hair, model accessory, state symbols, animations |
| `MangaGrammaire.dc.html` | how to read an avatar: states, models, projects, clones |

The board, the session detail, the journal and the compact window were designed on the same canvas with
real work data; they stay private and will be rebuilt here with invented fixtures.
